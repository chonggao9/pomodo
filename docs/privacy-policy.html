PomoDo Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026 | Effective Date: September 11, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: PomoDo is built with a Local-First, Privacy-by-Default principle. We do NOT collect, upload, track, monetize, or share your personal data, tasks, notes, or focus history. All your data stays strictly inside your device's private SQLite database.

1. Information Collection & Storage

PomoDo does not require an account, email address, or phone number to use. We do not maintain any cloud servers to store your personal productivity records.

  • To-Do Tasks & Notes: Stored strictly in local SQLite database on your device.
  • Pomodoro Sessions: Focus records and timestamps are stored locally.
  • Audio & White Noise: Ambient sounds are bundled offline within the application package; no internet streaming or telemetry is performed.

2. Third-Party Sharing & Analytics

PomoDo does not include any third-party tracking, profiling, or advertising SDKs (no Google AdMob, no Facebook SDK, no Firebase Analytics). Because no data is collected, no data is ever shared with or sold to third parties.

3. Device Permissions

The app requests only standard system permissions strictly necessary for timer execution:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides gentle haptic feedback on timer completion.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Keeps screen awake during focus countdown if opted in.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Delivers countdown alerts when running in background.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have full autonomy over your records:

  • Export: Export your full physical SQLite .db file via Settings -> Database Center.
  • Deletion: Delete individual tasks or reset the entire database directly in-app.
  • Uninstall: Removing the app permanently erases all locally cached records and database files from your device.
